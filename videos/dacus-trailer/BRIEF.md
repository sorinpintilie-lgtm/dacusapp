# Dacus app trailer

15-second trailer for the Dacus mobile app, in two versions: `index.html` here (9:16, 1080×1920) and
`../dacus-trailer-landscape/index.html` (16:9, 1920×1080). Both are generated by `build.mjs`.

## Concept

A fast, photo-driven cut on a 128 BPM grid, phone-free until the finale:
cold open (red roof line, TOT / PENTRU / CASĂ. hits, department photo strobe) → product tunnel with
"17.000+ produse" → the bulb card becomes the search bar ("led") → department photo slats that flip →
add to cart on red → loyalty card and points → silence → drop: Dacus lockup with three phones running
the real app and the products bursting around them.

## Assets

- `assets/store/`: collection photos and product images downloaded from the store's own Shopify CDN
  (`manifest.json` lists names and prices).
- `assets/screens/`: real app screenshots for the finale phones.
- `assets/fonts/`: Barlow, the app's typeface.
- `assets/audio/bgm.wav`: the score, generated by `music.py` from `timeline.json`.

## Rebuild

```bash
node build.mjs                 # both compositions + timeline.json (+ copies assets to the landscape project)
python music.py out.wav        # then loudness-normalise to -14 LUFS into assets/audio/bgm.wav
npx hyperframes render -q delivery -o "D:/TRAILERS/Dacus Trailer 9x16.mp4"
```

Delivered to `D:\TRAILERS\Dacus Trailer 9x16.mp4` and `Dacus Trailer 16x9.mp4`.
